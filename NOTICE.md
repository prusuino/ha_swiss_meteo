## Natural Earth

The outline of Switzerland and the lakes drawn by the map card are derived
from Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), 1:10m cultural and
physical vectors, which are in the public domain.

## MeteoSwiss open data

The forecast, station and pollen data come from the Federal Office of
Meteorology and Climatology MeteoSwiss under the terms of its open data
(Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch).
The MeteoSwiss weather-symbol graphics are proprietary and are not used;
only the symbol numbers and their meanings are.
